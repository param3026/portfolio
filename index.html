How I work, start to finish.

Every project follows this sequence. Each step feeds the next. Nothing ships without passing through all of them.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    I start by learning your business, your users, and your goals. I ask uncomfortable questions. I read your competitors' sites. I form a point of view before I open Figma.

    • Stakeholder interviews
    • Competitive audit
    • User research
  2. 02

    Direction

    I produce two or three divergent creative directions — moodboards, type pairings, motion references. We align on one direction before any pixel is placed.

    • Moodboards
    • Art direction
    • Motion language
  3. 03

    Design

    High-fidelity Figma designs with interactive prototypes. Responsive at every breakpoint. Every state defined — hover, focus, empty, error, loading.

    • UI design
    • Prototyping
    • Design system
  4. 04

    Development

    Production-quality code. Semantic HTML, optimised assets, smooth animations, accessible interactions. I write it as if it will run for ten years — because it usually does.

    • React / Next.js
    • GSAP + WebGL
    • Performance
  5. 05

    Launch

    I handle deployment, configure analytics, run Lighthouse audits, and train your team on the CMS. I don't disappear after handoff — I offer ongoing retainers for clients who want them.

    • Deployment
    • Analytics
    • Retainer option
